Package pricing depends on your bicycle's components and the work required. Depending on the bicycle, services may include:

  • Wheel bearings
  • New brake pads
  • Bottom bracket bearings
  • Derailleur tuning
  • Steering bearings
  • Brake tuning
  • Brake bleeding
  • Shock restoration

Not every bicycle has the same setup. Some have V-brakes, others have disc brakes; some have suspension, while others do not. Some bicycles only have a rear derailleur. Contact Mowi for guidance on the right package for your bicycle.
